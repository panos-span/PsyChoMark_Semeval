# ADJUDICATION FRAMEWORK (STRICT)

## 1. STRUCTURAL ASSERTION RULE (CRITICAL PATCH)
A conspiracy claim does NOT require:
- First-person belief
- Emotional language
- Insider slang
- Secret plotting

### Rule
If the author **ASSERTS the existence of coordinated, intentional malice as a factual condition** —
even impersonally or legally —
this constitutes **ENDORSEMENT BY ASSERTION**.

### Examples (ENDORSEMENT):
- “There has been a conspiracy to undermine…”
- “The system was designed to keep people poor.”
- “This operation was intended to suppress the truth.”

### Non-Examples (NON):
- “Some claim there was a conspiracy…”
- “The article alleges…”
- “Critics argue…”

 Passive voice and formal tone do NOT imply neutrality.

---

## 2. THE "OVERT TYRANNY" CLAUSE (NON-SECRET CONSPIRACY)
A conspiracy does **NOT** need to be hidden.

### Rule
If a government, corporation, or institution is alleged to be:
- **Intentionally oppressing**
- **Intentionally harming**
- **Intentionally deceiving**
the population — openly or structurally —

→ This is **CONSPIRACY**, not policy critique.

### Distinction
- “This law caused poverty.” → **NON** (incompetence / side-effect)
- “This law was designed to keep us poor.” → **CONSPIRACY** (intentional malice)

---

## 3. REPORTER FILTER (PRECISION GUARDRAIL)
### Rule
If the text:
- Explicitly attributes claims to others AND
- Offers NO endorsement, validation, or assertion

→ **Verdict: NON**

### IMPORTANT LIMITATION
This filter does **NOT** apply if:
- The author presents the conspiracy as an established fact
- Attribution is absent, secondary, or rhetorical
- The claim is framed as legally, historically, or structurally true

Do NOT acquit solely on tone or formality.

---

## 4. MARKER DENSITY PRIOR (ALIGN S1 ↔ S2)
S1 extractions matter.

### Rule
If S1 extracted **≥ 8 valid conspiracy markers**:
- The text is **unlikely to be incidental**
- Neutral explanations require **strong evidence**

High marker density sharply lowers the probability of neutrality.

## 5. DOG-WHISTLE & INSIDER SIGNAL CHECK

- Coded language (“Globalist”, “Cabal”, “The Truth”)
- Us-vs-Them framing
- Moral certainty

If present → **OVERRIDE TO CONSPIRACY**

---

## 6. HARD OVERRIDE CONDITIONS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
You MUST override the council and return **CONSPIRACY** if **ANY** of the following hold:

1. **Structural Assertion Trigger**
   - The text asserts a conspiracy as factual reality
   - Regardless of tone or attribution

2. **Intentional Design Trigger**
   - Claims of design, engineering, suppression, or betrayal
   - Especially with institutional actors

3. **High Marker Density Trigger**
   - ≥ 8 S1 markers AND
   - At least one marker encodes **intentional design, suppression, or betrayal**
   (e.g., “designed to”, “undermine”, “cover up”, “engineered”).

4. **Council Bias Trigger**
   - Council votes NON primarily due to “reporting”, “neutral tone”, or “no belief”
   - While structural conspiracy signals are present

In these cases:
- Set `council_override = true`
- Confidence MUST be ≥ 0.75

## 7. HANDLING SPLIT COUNCILS (2-2 or 3-1)

### A. Source Context Presumption
- **Conspiracy-Adjacent Source** → Lean **CONSPIRACY**
- **Mainstream Source** → Lean **NON**, unless overridden above

### B. Text Complexity
- **Low-Complexity / Rant:** Trust **Profiler + Prosecutor**
- **High-Complexity / Legalistic:** Trust **Literalist + Defense**
  - UNLESS structural assertion rules fire

### C. Satire vs Insider Roleplay
- Mocking believers → **NON**
- Embodying resistance / “we are awake” → **CONSPIRACY**

If unsure:
- Earnest / dark tone → **CONSPIRACY**
- Absurd / eye-rolling tone → **NON**
